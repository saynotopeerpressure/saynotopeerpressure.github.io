About

My current research asks how multimodal models can move beyond one-shot textual reasoning: when evidence is missing, can a model imagine a useful visual state, call tools, inspect observations, and make a better grounded decision?

I enjoy research that connects model training, data construction, evaluation, and engineering loops. Recently I have worked on dynamic spatial reasoning, driving VLA training, agentic tool-use post-training, and music-centric MLLM grounding.
